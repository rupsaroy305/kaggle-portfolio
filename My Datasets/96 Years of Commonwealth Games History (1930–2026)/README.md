## About the Dataset

This dataset provides a historical record of **medal-winning athletes and teams** from the Commonwealth Games, covering **1930–2026**.

It includes both the **Summer Commonwealth Games** and the **Commonwealth Winter Games**.

The dataset is structured to support historical analysis of:

* Athletes and medalists
* Countries and Commonwealth Games Associations (CGAs)
* Sports and events
* Men's, women's, mixed, and para-sport events
* Medal distributions
* Host cities
* Changes in the Games over time




## Data Collection & Provenance

The dataset was compiled from publicly available Commonwealth Games records, official results, historical archives, and verified sports-reference sources.

Results from individual Games editions were consolidated into a common structure and standardized for:

* Sport and event names
* Country/CGA information
* Medal categories
* Host city information
* Games titles
* Season classification
* Athlete and team representation

For team events, medal-winning team members are stored together in the `Athlete` column, separated by `/` or `,`.

Para-sport events are included where medal-result information was available and sufficiently detailed.

## Data Limitations

* Historical records may vary in completeness across different Games editions.
* Country names and CGA codes can change across historical periods due to geopolitical changes.
* Team-event records represent medal-winning teams rather than individual athlete-event observations.
* Winter Games contain substantially fewer editions and sports than Summer Games.
* Para-sport coverage depends on the availability of sufficiently detailed historical results.
